# How I work

[I like having a specific problem to work on.]{#start-with-a-real-question} On the [MCP project](/work/mcp-gateway), a lot of my time went into talking through reports with people who know the data. I wanted to know which table they’d open first and what they’d double-check before sending someone a number. That became part of the data dictionary the assistant uses.

[On the sign-in project,]{#find-out-what-has-to-keep-working} I started by going through the ways people used their accounts. Password recovery and guest Wi-Fi both went on the [list of things to check](/work/sso-nextgen).

[I use coding agents]{#review-the-change-where-it-runs} to try out an idea or get a first pass running. When I review the change, I like to open the app and try it myself. The [review workspaces](/work/review-workspaces) grew out of wanting to do that without putting my own work away.
